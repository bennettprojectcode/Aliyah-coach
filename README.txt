Aliyah Coach v2

Open index.html in Safari or Chrome.

The app is designed for phrase-level learning:
- Automatically cuts each of the four recordings into short practice phrases.
- Practice any phrase individually.
- Play a phrase three times.
- Hide the reference and record from memory.
- Receive feedback on timing, pitch steadiness and a conservative melody score.
- A phrase counts as learned after two good attempts.
- Progress is saved in the browser on the device.
- Build-up mode walks through the verse phrase by phrase.
- Cold-run mode lets you rehearse without the reference.

Microphone recordings are analyzed locally in the browser; they are not uploaded by this app.

For a fuller version, the next major upgrade would be adding the Hebrew text/transliteration and exact word-level alignment, which requires the text of the aliyah.
